WELCOME TO HISTORIC HYDERABAD

Hyderabad is one of the oldest and most historically important cities in Sindh, Pakistan. It was founded in 1768 by Mian Ghulam Shah Kalhoro and was named after Hazrat Ali (RA). The city became the capital of the Kalhora dynasty and later remained an important center under the Talpur dynasty. During the Talpur period, Hyderabad developed as an important center of trade, administration, and culture. The city played an important role in the history and political development of Sindh. Today, Hyderabad is known for its rich historical heritage and strong Sindhi cultural identity. Popular tourist attractions include:

  1. Pakka Qila
  2. Sindh Museum
  3. Mukhi House Museum
  4. Tombs of The Talpurs
  5. Shahi Bazaar
